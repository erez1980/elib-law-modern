## The project

`elib-law.co.il` — the website of **Elizabeth Barazani-Dadon**, an Israeli family-law
attorney and mediator based in Kadima-Zoran. The site is in **Hebrew, RTL**
(`lang="he" dir="rtl"`). Its job is to get a stressed person in the middle of a
divorce or family dispute to pick up the phone or send the contact form.

Stack and facts you should verify rather than assume:

- Astro 7.1.1, `output: 'static'`, `trailingSlash: 'always'`
- Deployed to **GitHub Pages** via `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` (push to `main`)
- Decap CMS (git-gateway) at `/admin`, `media_folder: public/images`
- CSP is configured in `astro.config.mjs` under `security.csp` (stable Astro 7 API,
  not `experimental.csp`) and is emitted as a `<meta>` tag with generated hashes
- Fonts are self-hosted via `@fontsource` (Assistant + Frank Ruhl Libre, Hebrew subsets)
- ~14 page files producing ~35 built routes (dynamic: `articles/[slug]`,
  `services/[slug]`, `areas/[slug]`), 14 articles, 21 FAQ entries
- Global CSS and the sitewide JSON-LD live in `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro`
- Internal links must go through `withBase()` in `src/utils/url.ts`

Start by running `npm ci && npm run build` and reading `dist/` — review the **rendered
HTML**, not only the source. There are no tests and no lint config; say so if that
matters to a finding.

## Deliberate decisions — do NOT recommend reverting these

Each of these was decided on purpose, some after a bug. Treat them as constraints.
If you think one is genuinely wrong, you may argue against it, but you must
acknowledge it was intentional and address the original reason.

1. **No new `is:inline` scripts and no inline `style=""` attributes.** Astro's CSP
   does not hash `is:inline` blocks, and style hashes do not cover style attributes.
   Enabling CSP already broke the site once this way (17 violations, dead theme
   toggle). The one `is:inline` tag that exists carries only a `src`.
2. **`public/js/prefs-init.js` is render-blocking on purpose** — it restores the
   theme and a11y prefs before first paint to avoid a flash. It is external (not
   inline) so CSP hashes don't need re-registering on every edit. Do not suggest
   `defer`/`async` or inlining it.
3. **HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy and
   Permissions-Policy are impossible** — GitHub Pages does not let you set response
   headers, and its cache TTL is fixed at 10 minutes. These need a CDN (Cloudflare)
   in front. You may note the gap once; do not file it repeatedly per page.
4. **`src/data/testimonials.ts` is deliberately empty** and the component renders
   nothing when it is. Inventing client quotes for a lawyer is deceptive and a bar
   association exposure. Never propose placeholder or sample testimonials.
5. **Court jurisdiction is deliberately absent from `src/data/locations.ts`** (see
   the header comment) — it could not be verified. Do not propose adding it.
6. The hero photo stays in `public/images` (not `src/assets`) because Decap CMS
   edits it, so it cannot go through Astro's image pipeline. Suggest other wins
   instead.
7. The Heebo font was removed on purpose. Do not propose adding fonts.
8. `trailingSlash: 'always'` plus `withBase()` is load-bearing — it fixed a Search
   Console "Page with redirect" problem across 24 internal links.

## What to review

### 1. Code

- Correctness and dead code. In particular check whether `src/components/Welcome.astro`
  is referenced anywhere, and flag any other unused component, data field, CSS
  variable, or image.
- Any internal link, canonical, sitemap entry or JSON-LD `url` that bypasses
  `withBase()` or lacks a trailing slash.
- Duplication across pages — the page files repeat a lot of scoped CSS and layout
  structure. Say which repetition is worth extracting and which is fine.
- Accessibility in the markup itself: heading order, landmarks, focus visibility,
  `aria-*` correctness on the accessibility widget, mobile menu and `<details>`
  FAQs, keyboard traps, contrast of the gold on dark in both light and dark theme,
  and whether `prefers-reduced-motion` actually disables every animation.
- Build warnings, TypeScript looseness (there are several `any`s in the dynamic
  routes), and the content-collection schema in `src/content.config.ts`.
- Performance of the built output: CSS/JS weight, render-blocking resources,
  LCP element, image dimensions and formats, layout shift risk. The last mobile
  PageSpeed score was 85 — say where the remaining points are.

### 2. Content (Hebrew)

- Read the 14 articles, the 21 FAQ entries, the 6 service pages and the 4 city
  pages. Judge them as a prospective client would, not as a word count.
- Flag: text that is vague or interchangeable between pages, promises or outcome
  guarantees a lawyer must not make, missing or inadequate legal disclaimers,
  anything that reads as legal advice rather than general information, inconsistent
  terminology, and Hebrew typos or grammatical errors.
- These are legal claims published under a named attorney. **Flag problems and
  quote the exact sentence — do not rewrite legal substance yourself.** Suggested
  wording is fine for marketing copy; for anything describing the law, say what is
  wrong and let the attorney decide.
- Check consistency of NAP (name, address, phone, email, hours) across the site,
  the footer, the JSON-LD and `src/data/site.ts`.

### 3. SEO

- Per-page `<title>` and meta description: uniqueness, length (titles were
  previously running to 92 chars), keyword placement, and whether they would earn
  a click in Hebrew SERPs.
- One `<h1>` per page, sensible H2/H3 structure.
- JSON-LD validity: `Attorney`/`LegalService` sitewide, `BlogPosting`, `Service`,
  `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`. Check required properties, that `@id` references
  resolve, and that FAQ markup matches visible on-page text.
- `robots.txt`, `sitemap-index.xml` (`/thank-you/` is filtered out on purpose),
  canonicals, OG/Twitter tags, `hreflang`/`lang`, 404 handling, the legacy
  redirects in `astro.config.mjs`.
- Internal linking: which pages are orphaned or more than three clicks from the
  homepage, and where contextual links are missing between articles, service
  pages and city pages.
- Local SEO for Kadima-Zoran, Netanya, Kfar Saba, Ra'anana, Hod Hasharon — are the
  city pages substantive enough to rank, or do they read as doorway pages?
- Keyword gaps: name the Hebrew family-law queries this site should rank for and
  currently has no page addressing. The site gets roughly 5 visits a day; be
  concrete about what would move that.

### 4. UX / UI design

- Open the built site in a browser at **390px (mobile) and 1440px (desktop)** and
  look at it. Chromium is available for Playwright.
- The brief for the design was "hybrid luxury, subtle and elegant motion". Judge
  whether it reads as a premium law firm or as a generic template — be specific
  about what creates or breaks that impression.
- Conversion path: how many steps and how much scrolling from landing to contact?
  Is the primary CTA obvious without being repeated into noise (CTA duplication was
  cut once already — check it hasn't crept back)?
- The contact form: field count, labels, error states, the required consent
  checkbox, the FormSubmit third-party notice, what happens after submit.
- Visual hierarchy, typographic scale, spacing rhythm, and RTL correctness —
  especially icons, arrows, breadcrumbs, and anything using `left`/`right` instead
  of `start`/`end`.
- The header logo and the mobile menu; dark mode across every page, not just the
  homepage.
- Any element that is decorative but costs attention, and any place where the
  motion system is noticeable rather than subtle.
